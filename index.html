About LOT 894 Tools

LOT 894 Tools is a free collection of practical online calculators & tools designed to make everyday calculations easier to understand and complete. The library covers finance, health, education, mathematics, construction, conversions, time, work, energy and other common tasks.

These calculators & tools are built to give you clear results quickly, with useful supporting information such as formulas, calculation details, explanations and report-ready summaries where available. You can search the calculators & tools library, filter by category, or browse the complete A–Z index.
